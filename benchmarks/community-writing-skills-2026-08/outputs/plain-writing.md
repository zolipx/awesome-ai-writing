Movie companies build sets so they can control the place, manage costs, handle problems, and keep people safe.

- Control. A set gives complete control over how the place looks and feels. This matters when the story is in a fantastical or fictional world, or when they want a specific mood or atmosphere.
- Cost. Filming on location can seem cheaper, but not always. Renting a place can be expensive, especially for a long time. They may have to pay to use certain places, and they may have to pay to change the place to fit the story.
- Logistics. Real locations can be hard to film in. There can be crowds, noise, and other distractions that make it hard to get the shots. The team may not have what they need there, like electricity, water, or special equipment.
- Safety. Sets can be safer. Real places can have traffic, unstable ground, or bad weather. On a set, the team can create a controlled space where these hazards are smaller.

Building sets can cost more at first, but they can be more cost-effective over time, because they give more control and reduce the risk of delays or unexpected costs.
